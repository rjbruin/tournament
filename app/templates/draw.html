The Draw

The 48 teams are split into 4 pots of 12. One team from each pot is drawn into each of the 12 groups (A-L). Hosts (Mexico, Canada, USA) are pre-seeded into A1/B1/D1. Spain/Argentina and France/England are placed in opposite halves of the bracket (A-F vs G-L). No group may have more than one team per confederation, except UEFA (which may have up to two).
